Honest note on strength
  The weakest implementations are the record-only setters: DFSP, DFSP_UCSA,
  PSF_DWELL, P2P_COEX, ELECTION_PARAMS, AWDL_ADVERTISERS. They parse the
  family's structure and publish it with Applied=false; no radio effect and no
  getter reads them back. That is deliberate (the driver has no firmware ABI
  for those BCM iovars) but it is observability, not control.
